Types of Flowers

Rose

Roses are one of the most famous flowers in the world. They are commonly associated with love and beauty. Roses can be found in colors such as red, pink, white, yellow, and orange.

Sunflower

Sunflowers are large, bright yellow flowers known for their tall stems and large flower heads. They generally grow best in sunny environments.

Tulip

Tulips are colorful flowers that are especially popular in gardens. They come in many colors, including red, pink, yellow, purple, and white.

Lily

Lilies are elegant flowers with large petals. They are available in several colors and are commonly grown in gardens.

Jasmine

Jasmine produces small, delicate flowers with a strong and pleasant fragrance. It is widely appreciated for its beautiful scent.

About Flowers

Flowers are the reproductive structures of flowering plants. They come in many different forms and play an important role in ecosystems by supporting pollinators such as bees, butterflies, and other insects.
